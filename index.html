Completing Open Enrollment in Rippling

Open enrollment is the window when most benefit choices lock in for the year. Miss the deadline and you usually wait until next year, unless you have a qualifying life event. Complete this guide before your enrollment task opens.

Before You Begin

Open enrollment appears as a task in your Rippling Inbox. Check your profile first, gather your dependents' details, and know your deadline. Then work through each benefit in order.

By the end of this training, you'll be able to:

  • Complete a benefits enrollment event from start to confirmation
  • Choose Healthcare FSA, Dependent Care FSA, and commuter options that fit your medical plan
  • Know when each change takes effect, and which changes are locked until next year
  • Change your election after you submit it, if your window is still open
  • Avoid the mistakes that cause people to lose coverage or money
🗓️
Section 1

Why This Matters

🗓️ Deadlines are final

If you miss open enrollment, you wait until next year to enroll unless you have a qualifying life event (QLE). FSA funds also have their own use-by and claim deadlines (Section 4).

👪 Dependents need your enrollment

You must enroll yourself before you can add dependent coverage. Dependents cannot be enrolled on their own.

📄 Waiving other coverage takes paperwork

If you waive because you're covered under a parent's or spouse's plan, Rippling asks you to upload documentation to finish the waiver.

💳 Pre-tax money has IRS rules

FSA and commuter funds come out of your paycheck before taxes. You can't withdraw cash from a commuter account.

🚫 One Healthcare FSA

Choose the Healthcare FSA or decline it. The Dependent Care FSA and the Commuter FSA are separate accounts.

🔒 Changes wait for the next window

Once you confirm, you can still change your election while the open enrollment window is open. After that, changes generally wait for a QLE, and new hire changes go through your admin.

⚠️ Key rule: Finish before your deadline. Finishing after your coverage start date still works if you're within the deadline, but a missed deadline does not reopen.

Benefits Enrollment: Open Enrollment or New Hire

Medical, dental, vision, and life coverage all run through the same enrollment task. Steps run in order, and each one shows what happens next.

The full sequence
  1. Confirm your benefits profile. Relationship status is Single, Married, or Living with a domestic partner.
  2. Enter dependents. You cannot enroll dependents unless you enroll too.
  3. Select your plans. To leave a dependent off, uncheck their name.
  4. Add life insurance beneficiaries (only if you chose Basic or Voluntary Life). Select Add Beneficiary, or Skip and return later.
  5. Complete the enrollee profile. Answer any carrier questions, such as Medicare coverage. Select Continue.
  6. Review and confirm. Select Edit to change the profile, then select Confirm.
  7. Sign any documents. Sign plan descriptions or waivers if Rippling asks.
  8. You're all set! 🎉 Nice work finishing open enrollment. Your elections are locked in for the plan year, and you can review them any time in My Benefits.
⚠️
Waiving coverage locks you in

A waiver stands until open enrollment or a QLE. If you waive because you have other coverage, upload the documentation Rippling asks for, or the waiver doesn't finish.

The confirmation pop-up that appears when you select Waive coverage.

✔️
After you confirm
  • You can reset and change your elections while the open enrollment window is still open. See Section 7.
  • Finishing after the effective date keeps your coverage start date. It does not move it.
  • View a summary of your election results in the Benefits app at any time after you select coverages.
  • Your benefits carrier sends instructions for accessing your ID cards after enrollment.
If you have a qualifying life event coming up

Make your elections based on your situation at the time you enroll, such as marriage, the birth of a child, or a move. After the event happens, submit a QLE in Rippling. It applies the coverage changes at that time.

How FSA money works

The training module's breakdown of the three FSA plan types.

The training module's explanation of grace periods.

⏳ Grace period

A period after the plan year ends when you can still incur new eligible expenses and use remaining prior-year funds. Not every plan has one.

📤 Runout period

The time after the plan year ends to submit claims for expenses from that year. For the 2026-2027 plan year, claims are due by January 31, 2028.

⚠️ Financial risk

Funds you don't use for eligible expenses can be forfeited at the end of the plan year or grace period, depending on your employer's plan design.

🚪
If you leave the company

The company owns FSA funds, and they're paid out only when an eligible transaction occurs. After you leave, you can't get FSA reimbursements for newly incurred medical expenses.

Submit a reimbursement claim

If your Flex card wasn't accepted for an eligible expense, pay out of pocket and submit a claim. Once it's approved, you're reimbursed for the amount you paid.

Select Submit claim at the top right of the Overview tab.

Choose the account the expense belongs to, such as the Healthcare or Dependent Care FSA.

  1. Open My Benefits. From the main sidebar, select Benefits, then My Benefits.
  2. Select Submit claim in the Overview tab, then choose your account: Healthcare FSA, Dependent Care FSA, or Commuter FSA (CommuteEase).
  3. Upload your receipt. Rippling scans image or PDF receipts and fills in the fields. Edit anything it gets wrong. Blank fields need manual entry.
  4. Submit the claim, then check its status in My Benefits › FSA › Transactions.

Mid-year changes
  • Submit a qualifying life event (QLE) in My Benefits. Rippling then creates an FSA task in your Inbox. You have 30 days from the QLE date.

  • Changes are allowed until the plan starts. After that, only a QLE allows changes.

Section 5

Commuter Benefits (Transit & Parking)

There is no enrollment window for commuter benefits. You can sign up or change your amounts any time, but changes follow a monthly cutoff. You can contribute up to $340 a month for transit and up to $340 a month for parking.

Scroll to Commuter in Current benefits and select Get started.

Make your elections
  1. Log in to your Rippling account and go to Benefits from your home screen.
  2. Select Commuter Benefits. It is sometimes listed under Flex Benefits.
  3. Enter your monthly Transit amount and your monthly Parking amount, up to $340 each.
  4. Save your changes. The updated deduction routes to your next scheduled payroll. Any amount above the IRS pre-tax limit is deducted after tax.
CommuteEase deadlines (Commuter FSA)

Current plan year: December 1, 2025 to November 30, 2026. The ITDP guide doesn't list commuter deadlines for 2026-2027, so confirm them with HR.

🚌
Vanpool, rail, and dependent rules
  • A vanpool qualifies only if it seats at least seven people including the driver, at least 80% of its mileage carries employees commuting, and commuting trips fill it to at least 50% of its seats.
  • Long Island Rail Road and Metro-North fares qualify.
  • Funds cover only your own commute. Card transactions or claims for a dependent's commute are declined.
Change your contribution
  1. Open My Benefits, then the Commuter section.
  2. On the Overview tab, select Change contribution next to the Transit or Parking account.
  3. Save your change. The Commuter section updates with the correct effective date.

Funds

Funds load on the first business day of each month. They're available by 6 AM Pacific and visible by 10 AM Pacific.

If your company adds money, you can split it between Transit and Parking. To receive the full employer amount, your total election must equal or exceed it. Funds can't move between accounts once deposited.

Section 6

Update a Dependent's Information

Name, date of birth, and SSN changes can be made any time. Adding or removing a dependent from coverage follows a different rule, covered after the steps below.

Your dependents list. Use Edit or Delete next to a dependent, or Add Dependent to add one.

The Add dependent form. Choose the relationship, then enter the dependent's details.

On the medical plan page, the Who is enrolling? checkboxes decide who is covered.

  1. Open your dependents. Benefits › My Benefits › Benefits profile › You and your Dependents.
  2. Select Edit next to the dependent's name.
  3. Make your changes and select Save.
  4. Select Confirm, review the changes, scroll down, sign, and select Continue.
⚠️
Limits
  • SSNs that start with 9 are not accepted.
  • Tobacco use can change only during an active enrollment event.
  • Adding or removing a dependent from coverage happens during open enrollment or after a QLE. Example: if a dependent gets coverage through their own employer, submit a QLE to drop them.
  • If your company uses a third-party broker and a dependent changed directly with the carrier, your admin can record the change.

Section 7

Change Your Election After You Submit

Once you submit, you can reset your enrollment and make changes as long as the open enrollment window is still open. Use the table to find the right path.

Your situationWhat to do
✅ Open enrollment hasn't endedReset your enrollment yourself and make the change. Follow the steps in the help article "Make changes to your open enrollment elections."
🔍 You can't see where to enrollSelect Benefits in the main sidebar. This opens My Benefits, where a notice at the top asks you to choose benefit plans. Select Enroll.
⏳ You're past the open enrollment deadlineAsk your administrator. They can tell you whether there's still time to make changes.
🆕 You're a new hire who submitted new hire choicesAsk your administrator to reopen them, if the enrollment deadline hasn't passed. Open enrollment elections you can reopen yourself. New hire enrollments must be reopened by an administrator.
💍 A QLE has happenedSubmit the QLE in Rippling within 30 days of the event. Admins can't submit it for you.
🔧
Admins can reset your enrollment

Your administrator can also reset your enrollment on your behalf. Ask them if you can't complete the reset yourself. This is a second route to the same result, not a change to the deadline.

Section 8

Mistakes That Cost Coverage

Most lost coverage comes from a short list of errors. Check this table before you confirm.

MistakeWhat to do instead
❌ Adding dependents before enrolling yourselfEnroll yourself first. Dependents can't be enrolled without employee coverage.
📄 Waiving because you have other coverageUpload the documentation Rippling asks for. Without it, the waiver doesn't complete.
🗓️ Missing an FSA deadlineWait until next year unless you have a QLE. Submit the QLE in My Benefits and finish within 30 days.
📅 Assuming Healthcare FSA money lasts past November 30, 2027Incur eligible expenses by November 30, 2027. Only up to $680 carries over.
🧾 Assuming the Dependent Care FSA ends November 30The grace period runs through February 15, 2027, with claims due February 28, 2027. Unused funds are forfeited after that.
🧮 Electing for a life event you expectElect based on your current situation. Submit the QLE after the event happens.
👶 Using a Dependent Care FSA for a child's medical billsUse the Dependent Care FSA only for care of a child under 13 or a household member who cannot care for themselves. Medical costs go through a Healthcare FSA.
🧾 Enrolling in a Dependent Care FSA without eligible dependentsEnroll only when you have eligible dependents and eligible expenses.
⏳ Waiting until after the deadline to ask about a changeAsk your admin while the window is open. After the deadline, they may not be able to help.
🛡️ Skipping life insurance beneficiariesSkipping is allowed, but add beneficiaries later in My Benefits.
🚗 Claiming rideshare, tolls, gas, or car maintenanceThese aren't eligible. Only transit fares and work-related parking qualify.
🧒 Claiming a dependent's commuteCommuter funds cover only your own commute. Those transactions and claims are declined.
🧾 Sending a receipt without a service dateReceipts must show the date of service or date range. If the date is missing, add the Rippling Flex claim form.
🏦 Submitting a claim before adding a bank accountThe Submit claim button stays disabled until a personal bank account is set up for reimbursement.
🧮 Bundling several transactions into one claimEach claim covers one transaction. File a separate claim for each.
🗺️ Using the Flex card outside the US, Canada, or MexicoThose charges are declined. Pay out of pocket and submit a claim when you return.
⏱️ Filing claims at the last minuteSubmit early enough to fix and resubmit a denied claim. See the claim rules in Section 4.
🚫
Don't rely on memory for deadlines

Missed open enrollment and FSA deadlines are final until a QLE. Put your deadline on your calendar the day your Inbox task appears.
